Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

hot-hott-yt-analytics is a personal tool used only by its owner. With the owner's consent it requests read-only access (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly) to the owner's own YouTube channel to read video statistics and analytics.

  • Data accessed: channel and video metadata and aggregate analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Use: performance reports for the owner only. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: reports and the access token are stored privately on the owner's own workspace. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The tool never uploads, edits, comments on, or deletes content.
